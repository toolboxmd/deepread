# Objective

Put Deep Read in front of real readers at deepread.toolbox.md and learn
whether people read deeply with it, at a cost per hour of reading that could
sustain the service.

The Objective is complete when:

- deepread.toolbox.md publicly serves the current reading experience: search,
  reading-paced generation, the Book layout, themes and text size.
- Spending cannot exceed the dedicated OpenRouter key's limit.
- Non-personal usage records show how far readers get in each article and what
  an hour of reading costs.
- A spot check of articles on real reader topics shows the model is accurate
  enough to keep, or it has been replaced.

Accounts, payments, saving or sharing articles, apps and SEO are outside this
Objective.
